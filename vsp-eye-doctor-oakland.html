VSP eye doctor in Oakland

Fruitvale Optometry is an in-network VSP provider in Oakland's Fruitvale district, next to the Fruitvale BART station. If your employer gives you VSP vision insurance, you can use it here for your eye exam, glasses and contact lenses.

What your VSP plan usually covers

Most VSP plans include a yearly comprehensive eye exam for a small copay, an allowance toward frames or contact lenses, and lens coverage for your prescription. Every plan is a little different, so we check your exact benefits before your visit and tell you what's covered and what any copay would be — no surprises at the front desk.

Using your VSP benefits here

  • No card needed. VSP doesn't issue cards — we look up your benefits with just your name and date of birth, so you can book today.
  • Comprehensive eye exams, contact lens exams, glasses and contact lenses, all in one visit.
  • Hundreds of frames in stock — Ray-Ban, Oakley, Saint Laurent, Balenciaga, Flexon and more — with your plan's frame allowance applied at checkout. See our frames & lenses.
  • On-site lab, so many glasses are ready fast.
  • Open 7 days a week, including Saturday and Sunday, next to the Fruitvale BART station.
  • English and Spanish spoken by every member of our staff.

Don't have VSP?

We're also in-network for EyeMed, Blue Cross / Blue Shield, Medi-Cal, Alameda Alliance and Medicare — see all the plans we accept. No insurance? We take cash, all major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and CareCredit.

Questions about your coverage? Call (510) 533-6567 and we'll look it up for you.
