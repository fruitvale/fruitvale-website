EyeMed eye doctor in Oakland

Fruitvale Optometry is an in-network EyeMed provider in Oakland's Fruitvale district, next to the Fruitvale BART station. If you have EyeMed vision insurance through work, you can use it here for your eye exam, glasses and contact lenses.

What your EyeMed plan usually covers

Most EyeMed plans include a yearly eye exam for a small copay, an allowance toward frames or contact lenses, and lens coverage for your prescription. Every plan is a little different, so we check your exact benefits before your visit and tell you what's covered and what any copay would be — no surprises at the front desk.

Using your EyeMed benefits here

  • Bring your EyeMed member ID if you have it — and if you don't, we can usually look your benefits up with your name and date of birth.
  • Comprehensive eye exams, contact lens exams, glasses and contact lenses, all in one visit.
  • Hundreds of frames in stock — Ray-Ban, Oakley, Saint Laurent, Balenciaga, Flexon and more — with your plan's frame allowance applied at checkout. See our frames & lenses.
  • On-site lab, so many glasses are ready fast.
  • Open 7 days a week, including Saturday and Sunday, next to the Fruitvale BART station.
  • English and Spanish spoken by every member of our staff.

Don't have EyeMed?

We're also in-network for VSP, Blue Cross / Blue Shield, Medi-Cal, Alameda Alliance and Medicare — see all the plans we accept. No insurance? We take cash, all major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and CareCredit.

Questions about your coverage? Call (510) 533-6567 and we'll look it up for you.
