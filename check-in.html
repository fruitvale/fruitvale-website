Why we ask: We text and email to tell you when your glasses or contacts are ready, to send appointment reminders, and to answer your questions.

Please know:

5 Retinal photos (optional — please choose YES or NO)

What is a retinal photo?

  • A quick, painless picture of the back of your eye (the retina).
  • Nothing touches your eye. It takes only a few seconds — like having your picture taken.
  • It usually does not require dilating drops — no blurry vision after your visit.

Why do we recommend it?

  • It helps your doctor find early signs of serious problems like diabetes, glaucoma, and macular degeneration — often before you notice any change in your vision.
  • Some eye problems cannot be seen without the photo.
  • Finding problems early can help prevent vision loss.
  • We recommend a photo once a year.
